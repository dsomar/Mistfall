## Description
Mistfall fills your screen with clouds of glowing light that never repeat.

Seventy thousand points of light drift, swirl and gather into seventeen patterns: a turning orb, ribbons, a murmuration of birds, a fountain, northern lights, pulsing rings, waterfall mist, a knot of light, a network of nodes, kaleidoscopes, flowing currents and Cipherfall, a rain of green dot-matrix characters. Colours and moods shift on their own, so it looks different every time.

USE IT AS YOUR SCREEN SAVER
Choose "Start When Idle" in the Mistfall menu and pick how long to wait. When you step away, Mistfall covers every display. Touch a key or the mouse and it is gone. Turn on "Open at Login" and it is always ready.

MAKE IT YOURS
- Show every pattern at random, changing as often as you like, or stay on one favourite
- Turn the soft glow on or off
- Make it livelier or calmer
- Let the lights follow your mouse in the window

SMOOTH AND LIGHT
Mistfall draws with your Mac's graphics chip, for fluid motion at 60 frames a second.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Free.

## Review notes (for Apple's reviewer)
Mistfall is an ambient light display. It does not install anything into the system.
To see the screen-saver behaviour: Mistfall menu > Start on All Screens Now, or choose
Start When Idle > After 1 Minute and leave the Mac untouched. Any key or mouse movement
dismisses it. "Open at Login" uses SMAppService and is off until the user turns it on.
